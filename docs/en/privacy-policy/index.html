TTP Foods Company Limited ("TTP Foods", "we") respects and is committed to protecting the personal data of customers, partners and website visitors. This policy explains what data we collect through ttpfoods.vn, why we use it, how we protect it and what your rights are, in line with Vietnamese personal data protection law.

2. Data we collect

Data you provide when you send the "Request a quote or advice" form: name, company, email, phone number, your need, product group of interest and your message. Required fields are marked (*); you may leave the others blank.

Data when you contact us directly by email, phone or Zalo: your contact details and what you tell us.

Technical data: the server hosting the website may automatically log IP addresses, browser type and access times for operation and security. We do not use this data to identify you.

We do not ask for or intentionally collect sensitive personal data through the website. Please do not send identity numbers, bank details or health data through the form.

The website is intended for businesses and professionals; it is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect data from, anyone under 16.

3. Purposes of use

  • Answering your questions and sending quotations, samples and advice on products and services you request;
  • Communicating, negotiating and performing contracts when we work together;
  • Operating, securing and improving the website;
  • Complying with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your data for advertising or marketing without your separate consent.

4. Legal basis & consent

When you send the form, you tick to agree that we may use the information to contact you, advise and send quotations. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us (section 12); this does not affect processing lawfully carried out before. In some cases we process data to perform a contract with you or because the law requires it.

5. Sharing & service providers

Only responsible TTP Foods staff can access your data. To run the website we use a few service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Web3Forms (Web3Creative, India; servers in the United States) – forwards the form you send to info@ttpfoods.vn. According to the provider, form contents are not stored; server logs are deleted periodically;
  • Our company email service – receives and stores contact emails;
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States) – hosts and delivers the website.

We disclose data to competent authorities only when required by law.

6. Transfers outside Vietnam

Data you send through the form passes through Web3Forms' servers in the United States before reaching our mailbox; technical data from visiting the website is processed by GitHub in the United States. We choose providers with appropriate security measures and complete the related procedures required by Vietnamese law.

7. Retention

We keep data for as long as needed for the purposes in section 3 – normally no longer than 24 months after our last contact if no business results – or longer where a contract or the law requires it (for example accounting records). After that, data is deleted or anonymised.

Processing starts when you send the form or contact us, and ends when the retention period above expires, or when you withdraw consent or ask us to delete your data (unless the law requires us to keep it).

8. Cookies & third-party services

  • The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are served from our own website, not from an external service.
  • The Google Maps map on the Contact page is only loaded when you click "Show map". Google may then collect data under its own policy.
  • The Zalo button and links to Google Maps open third-party services, which are governed by their own policies.

9. Security

The website uses encrypted HTTPS connections. We limit access to responsible staff and apply appropriate technical and organisational measures against unauthorised access, loss or leakage. If an incident occurs, we will handle and report it as required by law.

Possible risks: no system is completely secure. In a worst case, your contact details and request could be exposed if a mailbox or a service provider's system were accessed without authorisation, which could lead to spam or impersonation attempts. To limit this, we collect only what is needed, collect no sensitive data and limit how long we keep it. TTP Foods never asks for passwords, OTP codes or money transfers by email or message.

10. Your rights

Under personal data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about the processing of your personal data;
  • Give or refuse consent, and withdraw consent;
  • Access, view and request correction of your data;
  • Request deletion, restriction of processing and provision of your data;
  • Object to processing;
  • Complain, denounce, sue and claim damages as provided by law;
  • Protect your own lawful rights and interests.

To exercise these rights, please contact us (section 12). We may ask you to verify your identity first and will respond within 3 working days of receiving a valid request (or sooner where the law sets a shorter deadline).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the way we run the website changes or when the law changes. The new version applies from the time it is published on this page, with the version number and update date shown at the top. This policy is available in Vietnamese and English; if they differ, the Vietnamese version prevails.
