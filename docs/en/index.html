Services

Business consulting

We support food businesses from quality systems and plant operations to regulatory paperwork and new markets – saving time and cost while strengthening competitiveness.

  1. 01

    Food safety management systems

    We build and run ISO 22000, HACCP, GMP and other systems for food plants – from assessment, training and documentation through to certification.

  2. 02

    Factory optimization

    We assess your production, find bottlenecks, balance lines and raise equipment efficiency (OEE), with hands-on operator training.

  3. 03

    Quality declaration & product registration

    We handle declaration, self-declaration and registration dossiers end to end – from lab testing until the licence is issued, minimising legal risk.

  4. 04

    Food export promotion

    We help take your food products abroad, matched to local tastes, import regulations and your company’s resources.
